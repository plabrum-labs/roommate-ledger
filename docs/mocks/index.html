Roommate Ledger: two visual directions

Same data and the same five screens in each: dashboard, invoice roommates, Sam's statement, one invoice, one posting. Pick one.

Option A: paper ledger

A calm, single-column page on green-tinged paper with a red margin rule. Serif tabular figures, dot leaders between names and balances, accounting-style double-underlined totals, and credits in parentheses-style red ink. Status is a rubber stamp and settlement is a small fill gauge.

Option B: colour-coded app Chosen — revised

Revised after review: muted and square. A dark graphite sidebar on laptop and a bottom tab bar on phone. Each roommate is a neutral card with a muted tinted accent bar and number (ochre owes, green settled, slate-violet credit). 2px corners and 1px borders throughout. Invoices now carry editable line items and Invoice roommates creates one combined invoice per roommate. Status is an outlined tag with a shape icon; settlement is a tinted badge with a square progress bar.
